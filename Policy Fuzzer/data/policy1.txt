CASPIAN LOGISTICS LLC
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT AND BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Article 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets out how employees are reimbursed for business expenses and business travel. All amounts are in Azerbaijani manat (AZN).

Article 2. Approval of expenses
2.1 A single expense of up to 300 AZN may be approved by the employee's line supervisor.
2.2 A single expense above 300 AZN and up to 2,000 AZN requires the written approval of the department manager.
2.3 A single expense above 2,000 AZN requires the written approval of the Finance Director.
2.4 Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Article 3. Receipts and submission
3.1 An original receipt or invoice must be attached to every expense claim above 50 AZN.
3.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days. Late claims may be accepted with the consent of the department manager.
3.3 The same expense may not be claimed more than once.

Article 4. Business travel
4.1 Domestic business travel is permitted when it is necessary. The department manager decides whether a trip is necessary.
4.2 Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to 150 AZN per night.
4.3 A per diem of 60 AZN is paid for each day of travel to cover meals and incidental costs. No receipts are required for the per diem.
4.4 Air travel must be booked in economy class.

Article 5. Cash advances
5.1 An employee may request a cash advance of up to 1,000 AZN for a business trip. The advance must be settled after the trip by submitting receipts or returning the unspent cash.

Article 6. Client entertainment
6.1 Client entertainment is reimbursed up to 100 AZN per person, with the approval of the department manager.
6.2 The employee must record the names of the clients entertained.

Article 7. Policy owner
7.1 The Finance Department owns this policy and may update it at any time.
